Wake · on-device keyword spotter

It hears “Zed”, even when you mumble it.

A tiny speech model listens for one word, entirely on the phone. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere until it hears its name. Say it alone or inside a phrase; a built-in volume booster lifts soft or distant speech before the detector hears it.

Core · LiteRT-LM on-device runtime

Pick a brain that fits your phone.

ZED reads your phone's memory, processor and graphics chip, then recommends the model that will run best on it. Models run on the graphics chip when it's supported and fall back to the processor automatically. Every download is checked against its SHA-256 fingerprint.

Intelligence · three modes

Private by default. Powerful when you want.

Keep everything on the phone, or add your own key for an online AI and let ZED switch when it helps. Online models plan several steps on their own: find a contact, write the message, send it, then confirm what they did.

Local

Everything on the phone

Answers, actions, photos and voice without internet. Works on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere.

Routines · morning briefing · smart home

Your day, said like a person.

A daily briefing pulls in the weather, your calendar, reminders, open tasks and missed notifications, then reads it the way a good assistant would. Routines run on a schedule or when a Bluetooth device like your car connects, and can control a Home Assistant smart home.

Privacy · no servers · no accounts

Your phone is the whole system.

The MelHive Project runs no servers for ZED, creates no accounts and collects no analytics or advertising IDs. Conversations, memories and settings live in the app's private storage, and app backups are switched off. Something leaves the phone only when you use a feature that needs it.
