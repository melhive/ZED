Capabilities

A full assistant stack, on the device.

Wake word, speech, reasoning, memory and actions, all running locally and wired together to act the moment you speak.

Gemma, locally

Gemma 4 (or one of 12 other models) runs through LiteRT-LM on your phone's GPU or CPU. Answers, tool calls and photo understanding stay on the device.

gemma-4-e2b / e4b · litert-lm 0.19

Always-on wake word

A 3.3M-parameter keyword spotter listens for “Zed” on-device and opens a floating HUD over any app, with a spoken greeting.

sherpa-onnx kws · zipformer · 4 sensitivity levels

Neural voices, offline

Supertonic 3 and Kokoro render natural speech in a sandboxed process, sentence by sentence while the model is still writing.

streaming tts · separate :voice process

Acts immediately

Alarms, reminders, calendar, calls, SMS, files, media, Do Not Disturb, smart home. Requests run directly through system APIs, with no confirmation prompts unless you want them.

63 tools · schema-validated · audit log

Memory with a graph

Facts, relationships and documents form a local knowledge graph, the Live Brain. Recall is ranked by relevance to what you're saying now, not by what's recent.

room + fts · bm25 · relation extraction

Learns how you work

Correct it once and it remembers. Teach it rules (“keep it short”), pronunciations and shortcuts of your own. Every lesson can be reviewed and removed.

rules · pronunciations · aliases · corrections
Architecture

Deterministic first. Model when it matters.

Every request passes through three layers. Clear commands never wait for a language model, and everything the model does goes through the same validated tool pipeline.

  • Native parser handles everyday phrasings offline, in milliseconds, with no model loaded.
  • Typed tools: every action has a schema, argument validation, a risk level and an audit trail.
  • Small-model aware: Gemma sees only the few tools that fit each message, and malformed tool calls are repaired.
  • Focused context: only the current conversation and memories relevant to it reach the model.

Privacy

Your phone is the whole system.

There is no ZED server. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to sync. What you say is processed where you say it.

Stays on the device

Processed and stored locally, always.

  • Wake word detection and the microphone stream
  • Model inference: every answer and tool call
  • Speech synthesis
  • Memories, conversations, the Live Brain and indexed documents
  • Secrets in the Android Keystore

Leaves only when you ask

Explicit, visible network use.

  • Web research: only the search query, when you ask for it
  • Weather: your city or approximate location, to Open-Meteo
  • Smart home: to your own Home Assistant server
  • One-time downloads of models and voice packs
Specifications

What it runs on.

platform
Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer · 64-bit ARM
memory
4 GB RAM for Gemma 4 E2B · 8 GB for E4B
storage
2.3–3.3 GB for a model · ~130 MB per voice pack · 18 MB wake word
models
13 on-device models via LiteRT-LM: Gemma 4 E2B / E4B (GPU or CPU, with vision), Qwen 3.5, Phi-4 Mini, Ministral 3, DeepSeek R1 Distill, LFM 2.5, SmolLM2
speech
Supertonic 3 (31 languages), Kokoro, or the system voice · Android speech recognition
wake word
sherpa-onnx keyword spotting, on-device, adjustable sensitivity
stack
Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Room + FTS · WorkManager · built and signed on GitHub Actions
